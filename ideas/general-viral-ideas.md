# General viral video ideas (Kurzgesagt / Veritasium / Vsauce style)

## What makes these channels go viral
1. **"You" in the title.** The viewer is the main character: "What if *you*...", "How long would *you*...".
2. **A premise that only escalates.** Timelines, rankings and "every X" formats get more
   intense as they go, which keeps people watching to the end.
3. **Awe or dread.** The best-performing videos leave you feeling tiny, doomed or amazed.
4. **Repeatable formats.** One format turns into a series ("every planet" → "every ocean depth" → ...).
   This also means animation assets can be reused, which keeps a small team affordable.

---

## Ideas

### 1. "How Long Would You Survive in Every Era of Earth's History?" ⭐ top pick
- **Hook:** "Drop yourself onto Earth 4.5 billion years ago. You last 0.3 seconds. Let's move forward."
- **Beats:** Hadean (lava, no air) → Archean (no oxygen) → Great Oxidation → Snowball Earth →
  Cambrian (you finally breathe, but...) → Carboniferous (giant insects, 35% oxygen) →
  Permian extinction → dinosaurs → the asteroid → Ice Age → today.
- **Why it works:** a survival timer on screen that keeps going up gives built-in escalation.
  It's also a time-travel fantasy, full of surprising facts, and easy to turn into a series.
- **Sequels:** "...on Every Planet", "...at Every Ocean Depth", "...in Every Century of Human History".

### 2. "You Are 13.8 Billion Years Old"
- **Hook:** "The oldest thing you own isn't in your house. It's in your body."
- **Beats:** the hydrogen in your water comes from the Big Bang, the iron in your blood from dead stars
  and the calcium from supernovas. The atoms have been dinosaurs and oceans, and after you they'll be
  something else again.
- **Why it works:** it's pure awe, very shareable and hits you emotionally. It's the most "Kurzgesagt" idea on this list.

### 3. "What If You Lived for 1 Million Years?"
- **Hook:** "You can't die. At first it's great."
- **Beats:** year 100 (everyone you love is gone) → 1,000 (your language is dead) →
  10,000 (the stars you know have moved) → 100,000 (Niagara Falls is gone) →
  1,000,000 (new continents, maybe no more humans).
- **Why it works:** it combines immortality fantasy, a timeline, and existential dread with a lonely ending.

### 4. "Every Way the World Could End, Ranked by How Likely It Is"
- **Hook:** a tier list from "almost impossible" to "honestly, soon."
- **Why it works:** people love tier lists and people love arguing. The comments section fills itself,
  which drives the algorithm.

### 5. "What If Everyone Disappeared Except You?"
- **Hook:** "You wake up. 8 billion people are gone. What breaks first?"
- **Beats:** hour 1 (planes, cars) → day 1 (the power grid fails) → week 1 (the internet goes dark) →
  month 1 (fires, zoos) → year 1 → what still stands in 10,000 years.
- **Why it works:** the viewer is the main character, and it's a practical "what would I do" survival fantasy.

### 6. "Why Can't You Remember Being a Baby?"
- **Hook:** "You lived through three whole years that you'll never get back."
- **Why it works:** everyone experienced it and nobody knows the answer, which is a perfect curiosity gap.
  It's cheap to make and there's a lot of real science to cover.

### 7. "How Much Are You Worth?"
- **Hook:** "Your atoms are worth $X, your organs $Y, and the U.S. government says your life is $13 million."
- **Why it works:** it's slightly uncomfortable, starts from money, and the numbers shock people.

### 8. "The Invisible Machine That Keeps You Alive Every Morning"
- **Hook:** "Before you finish your coffee, 40 systems across 12 countries worked perfectly for you."
- **Beats:** water treatment, the power grid, GPS satellites, undersea cables and global supply chains,
  all shown through a single ordinary morning.
- **Why it works:** it's Wendover/Veritasium territory and changes how you see everyday life.

---

## Recommendation
Start with **#1**. It has the strongest built-in escalation, the clearest thumbnail
(you in a spacesuit with a survival timer reading "0.3 sec"), and it becomes a series
of at least four videos that reuse the same visual style.
**#2** is the best follow-up if we want an emotional, highly shareable piece.
