# Known stories, mysterious titles

These topics are **fully explained**, so we can tell the whole story with confidence. But the title and
cold open make the viewer feel they've stumbled onto a mystery. The formula:

> **Strange fact in the title → "how is that possible?" → the real explanation revealed at the end.**

The title never gives the answer away. Each entry has the title, the mystery hook, and the real answer we
reveal. Check every number before scripting.

## Top picks

### 1. "1,746 People Died in One Night and Nobody Heard a Thing" ⭐
- **Hook:** A whole valley of villages in Cameroon was found dead one morning in 1986. There was no fire,
  no wounds and no sound, and animals were lying where they fell.
- **Reveal:** Lake Nyos. Carbon dioxide had built up at the bottom of the lake for centuries, then burst out
  all at once. The heavier-than-air gas flowed downhill and suffocated everyone in its path.
- **Why it works:** a horror-movie opening with a real science answer, and it's very visual (an invisible
  gas cloud rolling down the valley).

### 2. "The Town That Has Been on Fire Since 1962" ⭐
- **Hook:** Smoke rises from cracks in the road. The town has emptied out, and only a handful of people
  are left.
- **Reveal:** Centralia, Pennsylvania. A fire got into the coal seam underneath the town and nobody could
  put it out. It's still burning.

### 3. "The Island Where Everyone Sees in Black and White" ⭐
- **Hook:** On one tiny Pacific island, about 1 in 10 people are born completely colorblind. Elsewhere it's
  roughly 1 in 30,000.
- **Reveal:** Pingelap. A typhoon in the 1770s left about 20 survivors, one of whom carried the gene.
  Genetics then did the rest.

### 4. "The Man Who Survived Both Atomic Bombs"
- **Hook:** He was in Hiroshima on August 6, 1945, and went home three days later. Home was Nagasaki.
- **Reveal:** Tsutomu Yamaguchi, who lived to 93.

### 5. "Your Diamond Is Worth Almost Nothing"
- **Hook:** Try to sell a diamond ring back and see what you're offered.
- **Reveal:** De Beers controlled the supply, and a 1947 ad campaign ("A Diamond Is Forever") invented the
  engagement-ring tradition as we know it.
- **Why it works:** angry, shareable, and it ends with "wait, what else is like this?" comments.

### 6. "The War Australia Lost to Birds"
- **Hook:** In 1932 soldiers with machine guns were sent into battle, and lost.
- **Reveal:** the Emu War.

### 7. "A Man Ate Nothing for 382 Days"
- **Hook:** He didn't eat for over a year, and he survived.
- **Reveal:** Angus Barbieri's medically supervised fast (1965–66). He went from 207 kg to 82 kg. The video
  explains what the body actually does when it runs on its own fat.

## Recommendation

Start with **#1 (Lake Nyos)**. It has the strongest mystery hook and a satisfying science reveal, and it fits
the animation style we've built: the invisible gas cloud flowing down the valley is a great shader shot.
**#2 (Centralia)** and **#3 (Pingelap)** are close behind. All three work as a Shorts cut too.
